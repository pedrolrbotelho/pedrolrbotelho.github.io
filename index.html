  • Short Bio

  • I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, working with Prof. Guannan Qu on the foundations of multi-agent reinforcement learning — in particular, Locally Interdependent Multi-Agent Markov Decision Processes (LIMAMDPs). I am also a visiting student in the Communications and Networking Laboratory at Northwestern University, working with Prof. Igor Kadota, and a research scientist at the Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence (CEIA), Brazil. I received my Bachelor of Science in Electronic Engineering from Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica - ITA, Brazil’s top-ranked engineering school (acceptance rate of 0.98%).

    My research interest lies in the intersection of reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and robotics. I’m interested in how multiple learning agents can coordinate robustly and efficiently through scarce and unreliable communication. I have been committed to understanding the interplay between intelligent systems, other agents, and dynamic environments.

    I believe that grounding theoretical foundations in scalable and reliable methods in reinforcement learning, robotics, and dense multi-agent systems can address limitations by giving agents principled ways to make decisions and coordinate under partial observability and uncertainty.

    Feel free to contact me if you are interested in working on robotics or (multi-agent) reinforcement learning!
